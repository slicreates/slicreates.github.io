What it does

Pick the drive layout, motors, inverters and battery cells or modules, set the reduction, wheels, weight and aero, and get indicative figures straight away.

  • Performance: top speed, 0-100 km/h, 80-120 km/h and ¼ mile from a full-throttle simulation, for rear, front or all-wheel drive with one or two motors per axle. Traction limits per axle with weight transfer, sustained top speed and hill climbing.
  • Range: WLTP, EPA city and EPA highway drive cycles with regenerative braking, an EPA window-sticker estimate, and range at your cruising speeds.
  • Charging: DC fast charging with taper (10-80% time, power curve), AC charging time, and battery temperature with passive or liquid cooling.
  • Dynamics: axle loads, grip-limited acceleration for FWD, RWD and AWD, braking limits and regen deceleration.
  • Component library: built-in drive units, inverters, cells and modules, plus your own. Save builds and compare them side by side.

Metric and imperial units. Works offline. No accounts, ads or tracking.

Results are indicative estimates from simplified vehicle-dynamics models and standard drive cycles. They are for planning, not a substitute for engineering verification and testing.
