EV Kart Calc – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

EV Kart Calc ("the app"), listed on Google Play as EV Kart Calculator, is published by Sli Creates. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the app stores

  • Your builds and component library. Motor, controller, battery and kart details you enter, and the settings you choose (for example units), are saved in a file in the app's private storage on your device. They are used only to show you calculation results.
  • This data never leaves your device. The app does not upload, sync or back it up anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include it if you have backups turned on. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

The app is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Sli Creates does not receive your card or payment details. Google provides only aggregated sales reports and the order information it requires for payouts and refunds.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not:

  • ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there are no accounts)
  • access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other device data
  • use advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking
  • include analytics or crash-reporting services that send data off your device
  • make network requests during normal use

Children

The app is a general-audience engineering tool. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
