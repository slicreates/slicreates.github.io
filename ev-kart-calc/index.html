What it does

Pick a motor, controller and battery cells, set your sprockets, tyres and weight, and get indicative figures straight away.

  • Performance: top speed, 0-50 and 0-80 km/h, corner-exit 40-80 km/h, the first 100 m, power to weight and the rear-axle grip limit.
  • Sprocket sweep: find the rear sprocket for the best acceleration or top speed.
  • Battery: pack builder, voltage sag, run time, current and cell temperature at different power levels, and charging time.
  • Classes: presets from leisure karts to professional classes, with minimum weight, power and voltage checks.
  • Dynamics: weight distribution, braking with rear-only or all-wheel brakes, and corner speed by radius.
  • Component library: built-in motors, controllers and cells, plus your own. Save builds and compare them side by side.

Metric and imperial units. Works offline. No accounts, ads or tracking.

Results are indicative estimates from simplified vehicle-dynamics models. Class limits are typical values: always check your series' current rules. They are for planning, not a substitute for engineering verification and testing.
