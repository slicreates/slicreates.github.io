What it does

Pick a motor, controller and battery cells, set your gearing, wheels and weight, and get indicative performance figures straight away.

  • Performance: top speed, 0-100 km/h, ¼ and ½ mile from a full-throttle simulation, launch limits from tyre grip and wheelies, sustained top speed and hill climbing.
  • Gearing sweep: find the rear sprocket for the best acceleration or top speed.
  • Battery and range: pack builder, voltage sag, range at your cruising speeds, charging time and battery temperature with passive cooling.
  • Dynamics: weight distribution, traction, wheelie and braking limits.
  • Component library: built-in motors, controllers and cells, plus your own. Save builds and compare them side by side.

Metric and imperial units. Works offline. No accounts, ads or tracking.

Results are indicative estimates based on established vehicle-dynamics methods (Cossalter, Motorcycle Dynamics). They are for planning, not a substitute for engineering verification and testing.
