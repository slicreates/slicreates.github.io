EV Moto Calc – common questions

Why don't the results match my real bike?

EV Moto Calc gives indicative estimates from simplified vehicle-dynamics models. Real results depend on accurate component data (especially motor torque curves, controller current limits and cell ratings), tyres, rider technique and conditions. Check the component values in your build. Notes marked "Estimated" show where the app has had to estimate a value.

A component's figures look wrong

Built-in parts use published or commonly quoted figures, which sometimes vary between sources. You can edit any value in your build, or in the Library. Settings → Restore built-in library items brings the originals back.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your builds. See the privacy policy.
